Lab 5 helped me understand that each part of a robot, like the chassis, sensors, and wheels, needs its own coordinate frame so its position and orientation in 3D space can be defined. These frames are connected in a hierarchical tree structure, where every frame has one parent. This system gives the robot a mathematical way to determine how different parts relate to each other, such as figuring out the position of a sensor or measuring the distance from that sensor to an obstacle in the world frame.
