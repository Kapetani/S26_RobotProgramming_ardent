In this activity, I learned how coordinate frames, joints, and axes interact to build a 3D model. Adjusting origin values demonstrated that visual geometries and child links are always positioned relative to their parent frames. For example, when moving the lid's joint to the back wall, I had to shift the lid's visual origin by 0.5 units to keep it properly aligned with the box. Finally, setting up the revolute hinge highlighted the importance of the right-hand rule; I had to invert the rotation axis to <axis xyz="0 -1 0"/> so the lid would open upward naturally instead of swinging downward into the box.
