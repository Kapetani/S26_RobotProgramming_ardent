Through this activity, I got a better understanding of how URDF uses coordinate frames to define the position of each link. I learned that the joint origin controls how links are connected, while the visual origin is used to adjust the geometry inside each link. Building the box step by step helped me understand how complex structures can be created from simple components. I also experimented with a revolute joint for the top link, which showed how motion can be added and controlled using the joint state publisher.
