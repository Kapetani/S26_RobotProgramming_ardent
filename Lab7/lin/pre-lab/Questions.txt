This node functions as a ROS2 driver for a differential-drive robot in Webots. It subscribes to the cmd_vel topic and converts linear.x and angular.z commands into left and right wheel motor velocities. As a result, the robot can move forward, backward, turn left, turn right, and follow curved paths. The main purpose is motion control.
