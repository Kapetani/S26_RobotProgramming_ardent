This node acts as the motion-control driver for a differential-drive robot in Webots using ROS 2. It listens to velocity commands from the cmd_vel topic and translates the linear.x and angular.z values into separate left and right wheel speeds. Because of this, the robot is able to drive forward and backward, rotate left or right, and move along curved paths. In short, the node’s function is to control the robot’s movement based on incoming velocity commands.
